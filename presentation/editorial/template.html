Q&A appendix

How did you prevent leakage?

48,000 training images
Fit the classifiers. Divide raw pixel intensities by the known maximum of 255; this uses no estimated statistics.
12,000 validation images
Choose C, k, CNN settings and checkpoint. All models use identical source indices. Only the first split from StratifiedGroupKFold was used.
10,000 official test images
Freeze all three checkpoints, verify their hashes and validation predictions, then score once. Exact development/test overlaps: zero.

Q&A appendix

How was kNN training accuracy measured?

1 · Exclude the query
For each of the 48,000 fitted images, exclude its own index and search the other 47,999 images.
2 · Keep the selected voting rule
Three Euclidean neighbours, one vote each. A three-way tie goes to the lowest class ID, matching the classifier.
3 · Count correct votes
40,931 ÷ 48,000 = 85.27%. Validation: 85.38%. Test: 85.04%. These close scores give little evidence of a large generalisation gap.

Why keep the epoch-12 CNN?

Within a run
Validation loss reached its minimum at epoch 12. Training stopped at epoch 17 and restored that checkpoint.
Across configurations
Validation accuracy chose the wider CNN without weight decay. The original seed-42 checkpoint was already selected.
Across three seeds
Mean accuracy: 92.400% without weight decay and 92.339% with it. Mean shirt recall favoured weight decay: 78.94% vs. 75.81%. All runs used one split.

Q&A appendix

What do these SHAP values measure?

Quantity explained
The frozen CNN’s Coat probability. One feature is a 14 × 14 quadrant. Absent quadrants come from the same reference image.
How the values were computed
Evaluate all 16 subsets of the four quadrants, averaging each over 20 training images (two per class). Average each region’s marginal change over possible orders.
How far the explanation travels
The baseline plus four signed contributions recovers each prediction. The beeswarm covers ten validation images per class. Other backgrounds or region boundaries can change the values; mixed images may look artificial.
