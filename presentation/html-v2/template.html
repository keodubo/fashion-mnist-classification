11 / LOOK AT THE FAILURES

Shirt recall: 67.6%.

676 of 1,000 actual shirts correctly identified. Each arrow reads actual → predicted.

OBSERVED

Balanced classes.
Lower shirt recall.
More errors from shirts into these classes than in reverse.

PLAUSIBLE EXPLANATIONS

Overlapping silhouettes.
Limited detail at 28 × 28.
Potentially more variation within shirts.

Causes not proven.
Within-class variation not measured.

LARGEST CONFUSIONS

Similar silhouettes and low detail are plausible explanations.

They do not prove why an individual prediction failed.

Equal class sizes do not imply equal difficulty. Errors need not be symmetric. Each direction starts with 1,000 actual images.

12 / WHERE THE EVIDENCE STOPS

A strong benchmark result.
A limited claim.

01

Benchmark images

Phone photographs, new lighting and backgrounds remain untested.

02

One validation split

Repeated choices can overfit development evidence. Three seeds do not replace cross-validation.

03

Several factors changed

Capacity and training also differ. This comparison does not isolate the effect of convolution alone.

04

Frozen evaluation

No exact development/test overlaps. No refit on all 60,000 development images.
